Fixing the KYC back and forth in commercial onboarding

A business client uploads every document and the account still is not open. This case study finds out where the days go, decides what to fix first, and shows the fix in a clickable prototype.

The problemThe first request says “incomplete”. Nobody knows what to fix or who acts next.
The decisionOne structured request: the items, the reasons, one owner, one date.
The metricRepeat-request rate, which feeds median days to KYC clearance.

How the work flows

Each step answers one question and hands its result to the next.

  1. DiscoverySplit every day of delay into four buckets before choosing a fix.
  2. ValidationFour checks, with pass or fail rules set before looking at the data.
  3. PrioritizationWeighted scoring, a stability check, then MoSCoW and dependencies.
  4. AlternativesWhat was rejected, and what each choice gives up.
  5. SolutionA reviewer workspace. AI is limited to one step and a person decides.
  6. MeasuresOne north star, leading and lagging metrics, and a guardrail on errors.
