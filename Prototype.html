One request, one owner: fixing the KYC back and forth

A business client has opened an account application to close a deal on 30 November. The documents are in. The account is still not open. Switch between the current process and the improved one, click through five steps, and watch where the days go.

Who it is forThe onboarding specialist, and the business client waiting on them.
The problemThe first request says “incomplete”. Nobody knows what to fix or who acts next.
The decisionReplace free text with one structured request: items, reasons, one owner, one date.
The metricRepeat-request rate. How often a client is asked twice for the same gap.
